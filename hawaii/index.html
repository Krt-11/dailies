Hawaiʻi,
done right.

A seven-night adventure for 4–5 people, built around volcanic landscapes, tropical rainforest, giant waterfalls, high-altitude sunsets, canyon country and the Nā Pali Coast. Designed for a group of young adults who would rather hike at sunrise than sleep in.

The route

CMH → KOA · Hawaiʻi Island → LIH · Kauaʻi → CMH

Two islands is the sweet spot for this trip. Hawaiʻi Island gives you volcanoes, rainforest and Mauna Kea; Kauaʻi gives you the deep green canyon and Nā Pali Coast scenery. The itinerary deliberately avoids spending the trip bouncing through Honolulu.
